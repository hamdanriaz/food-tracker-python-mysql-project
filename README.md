## 🛠️ Features
**READ DISCLAIMER IN THE BOTTOM FIRST**

Here is what you can do with Food Tracker:
- Create an account for yourself, that contains your data (like height, weight, age, and your activity level) which is used to calculate your [Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_expenditure) which is basicaly the amount of calories your body burns, just to keep you alive
- You can enter the food you ate and the amount (in grams) which will then be converted into calorie intake and will add up to total calorie intake which can be noted at the end of the day
- You can also add any food you can't find on the database, by entering the food name and the calories per 100g
- Once finished, you can see the remarks below referenced from your total calorie intake
- The program offers variety of other features that is referenced from you calorie intake
- One such feature is the `AI Diet Advice` which gives you advice for each food that you had <sub>Note: The AI might not work properly and is an expermintal feature... You are more than welcome to work on the AI in order to make it better.. and maybe even send it to me so WE can fix the AI</sub>
- Another Feature is the `Generate Graphs` which uses Matplotlib to generate graphs on a timeframe.. You can create a graph on daily, weekly, monthly and even yearly basis.. the feature even allows you to save the graph as an image
- Finally, the `Download Report` which saves a report of your food intake (as pdf) on that day
- There is also `Settings` which lets you change themes and reset your data
